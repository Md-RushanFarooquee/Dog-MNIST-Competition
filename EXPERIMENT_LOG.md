## Competition constraints (source: README.md / README.pdf)

- Train only on the 1,979-image train set. No pretrained weights, transfer learning, fine-tuning, distillation from a pretrained model, or pretrained embeddings. No external or generated data. Augmentation is allowed.
- sample-test (48 images) is a smoke test only. Never train, validate or tune on it.
- Ranking: accuracy on the hidden test set (445 images). Ties go to the fewest parameters (frozen ones count too). For nn.Module the evaluator counts parameters.
- Submission folder: predict.py (load_model, predict, count_parameters only for non-PyTorch/Keras models), weights, training notebook or train.py, requirements.txt. Offline, CPU, relative weight paths, all preprocessing inside predict().
